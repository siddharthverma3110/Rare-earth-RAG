# Rare Earth Elements Overview (placeholder)

Rare earth elements (REEs) are 17 metals: the 15 lanthanides plus scandium and yttrium. They split into light (LREE: La to Sm) and heavy (HREE: Eu to Lu, plus Y). Despite the name they are relatively abundant in the crust; the difficulty is finding economically concentrated, mineable deposits.

## Magnet applications

Neodymium (Nd) and praseodymium (Pr) are key to NdFeB permanent magnets used in EV motors and wind turbines. Dysprosium (Dy) and terbium (Tb) are added to raise coercivity at high temperatures.
