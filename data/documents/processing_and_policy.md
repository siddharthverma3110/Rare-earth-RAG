# Processing, Supply Chain and Policy (placeholder)

Processing involves mining, beneficiation, cracking, solvent-extraction separation, and metal or alloy making. Separation, especially of heavy REEs, is the most concentrated step globally and the main supply-chain bottleneck.

Mining and processing can generate radioactive tailings (thorium, uranium) and acidic waste. Governments are pursuing recycling, stockpiling, and allied supply chains to reduce import concentration risk.
